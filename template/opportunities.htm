Research Opportunities

We welcome enquiries from motivated candidates interested in environmental microbiology and microbial nitrogen cycling. Explore our research themes to learn more.

PhD Students

We welcome prospective PhD students with backgrounds in microbiology, environmental science, environmental engineering, or related fields, and an interest in microbial nitrogen cycling in natural and engineered environments.

Postdoctoral Fellows

Candidates with a relevant PhD are welcome to discuss research opportunities. Experience in microbial ecology, molecular biology, biogeochemistry, or bioinformatics is particularly relevant to our work.

Research Assistants

We welcome enquiries from candidates with a relevant bachelor’s or master’s degree who are interested in supporting laboratory experiments, data analysis, and day-to-day research activities.

Please email Prof. Penny Han at pinghan@cityu.edu.hk with your CV and a brief description of your research interests, indicating the role you are interested in.
